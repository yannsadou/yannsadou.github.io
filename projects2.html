Research Interests

Space Systems Architecture
Digital-twin microgrids for Moon & Mars – Politecnico di Milano
Systems
  • Design of hybrid surface power systems for human-class planetary outposts.
  • Digital-twin modeling of power generation, distribution, storage and ISRU integration.
  • Architecting resilient lunar/martian microgrids under extreme environmental constraints.
  • Multi-physics simulation of mission-scale energy flows and load profiles.
  • Optimization of autonomy, reliability and fault-tolerance of surface infrastructures.
EDL & Power Systems Architecture
Human Mars lander – European Space Agency (SEEDS Program)
Trajectory & EPS
  • End-to-end design of guided entry, descent, and landing trajectories for a Human Mars Lander mission concept.
  • Trajectory-driven sizing and performance assessment of aeroshells, inflatable decelerators, and terminal descent propulsion.
  • Mission-level Electrical Power System (EPS) architecture and power budget from cruise to surface operations.
  • Trade-off studies and implementation concepts for RTGs, solar arrays, and fuel-cell solutions to secure take-off, EDL and early surface power needs.
Commercial Orbital Infrastructure
European Commercial Space Station in LEO – European Space Agency (SEEDS Program)
Space Strategy
  • Techno-economic assessment of a fully European commercial LEO station.
  • Cost modeling, market forecasting, and roadmap definition for crewed operations.
  • Benchmarking against ISS, Axiom, Starlab and international commercial players.
  • Identification of strategic ESA/NASA/industry partnerships for sustainable deployment.
CMB Polarimetric Instrumentation
BICEP Array – Caltech
Space Instrumentation
  • Mechanical integration and validation of mm-wave focal plane modules.
  • Micro-wirebonding of TES RF Readout.
  • Cryogenic interfaces for large-format TES bolometer arrays.
  • Design of optical support structures and alignment of cold optics elements.
  • Module-level testing, characterization, and readiness verification for South Pole deployment.
  • Coordination with Harvard, Stanford, UBC, and Minnesota for multi-institution hardware development.
Dark-Matter Direct Detection
Cryogenic phonon-mediated detectors – Caltech
Space Instrumentation
  • Development of sub-Kelvin detector architectures for low-mass dark-matter searches.
  • Thermal system design for multi-stage dilution refrigeration cryostats.
  • Optimization of mechanical assemblies for extreme black-body radiation isolation and low-noise operation.
  • Integration of phonon-sensing elements with readout electronics in vacuum environments.
  • Contribution to next-generation detector concepts bridging astrophysics & particle physics.
Astronomical Instrumentation
Microwave Kinetic Inductance Detector (MKIDs) Developement– Caltech
Space Instrumentation
  • Development of large-format MKID arrays for ground-based mm/sub-mm astronomy (Caltech Submillimeter Observatory).
  • Integration of detector wafers into cryogenic optical benches for the CSO and prototype systems.
  • Design of mechanical/thermal interfaces for ultra-stable resonator performance.
  • Optimization of optical coupling architectures and cold alignment strategies.
  • End-to-end module assembly and system-level test campaigns for instrument prototypes.
